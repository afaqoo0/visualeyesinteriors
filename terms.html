2. Design Scope & Engagement Process

VisualEyes Interiors specializes in high-end residential, commercial, and executive interior design solutions across Peshawar and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Our engagements follow structured phases:

  • Initial Consultation & Feasibility: Preliminary meetings to assess project requirements, spatial dimensions, and functional goals.
  • Concept Design & Spatial Planning: Mood boards, 2D floor plans, architectural space planning, and 3D conceptual visualizations.
  • Detailed Specifications: Material schedules, joinery details, lighting layouts, and itemized financial estimates.
  • Execution & Turnkey Delivery: On-site procurement, artisan oversight, and quality compliance according to agreed milestones.

3. VisualEyes Triple Guarantee Terms

Our promotional Triple Guarantee applies to formal turnkey contracted projects:

  • Bespoke Originality: All custom residential concepts are originated for the specific client. We do not duplicate complete bespoke designs across projects.
  • Architectural Quality Assurance: If custom joinery or workmanship completed directly by our verified craftsmen does not meet agreed structural specifications upon inspection, adjustments will be rectified under our supervision.
  • Dedicated Concierge: Every turnkey project is assigned a designated single point of contact available during standard operational hours (Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM PKT).
