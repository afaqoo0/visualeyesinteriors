Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • VisualEyes Interior Design Services, Peshawar, Pakistan

High Standards of Client Privacy & Non-Disclosure

1. Commitment to Privacy

At VisualEyes Interiors, we respect the confidentiality of our clients, homeowners, and commercial partners. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, safeguard, and disclose your personal and spatial project data across our consultations, architectural workflows, and digital interactions.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a design cost estimate, or commence an interior design commission, we may collect:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, phone number, WhatsApp contact, email address, and billing information.
  • Property & Spatial Specifications: Residential address, site location in Peshawar (e.g., DHA, Hayatabad, University Town), architectural blueprints, floor measurements, and property photographs.
  • Design & Aesthetic Preferences: Style questionnaires, budget parameters, lifestyle requirements, and material requests.
  • Technical Website Data: IP address, device type, browser settings, and page interaction timestamps gathered to optimize website performance.

3. How We Utilize Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate project delivery purposes:

  • Generating customized interior design estimates, concept proposals, and budget models.
  • Coordinating on-site measurements, structural walkthroughs, and material deliveries.
  • Facilitating direct communication with your dedicated Project Concierge.
  • Managing trade orders and bespoke joinery production with verified artisans under non-disclosure obligations.

4. Non-Disclosure & Client Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing firms or data brokers. All client residence floor plans, security configurations, and private residential layouts are handled with strict discretion.

Before publishing any completed project images in our public portfolio or marketing campaigns, we respect client preferences and never publicize home security features or exact residential addresses.

5. Data Security Measures

We employ administrative and digital safeguards to protect your files, architectural CAD drawings, and contact records against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

6. Your Rights & Access

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to your project files, or ask to withdraw marketing consent at any time.
