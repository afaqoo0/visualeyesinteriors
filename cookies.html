Transparent Tracking & Browser Storage Information

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are compact text files placed on your device when you browse websites. They are widely used to ensure websites function smoothly, remember your custom viewing preferences, and provide analytical data to website operators.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

VisualEyes Interiors uses cookies and local browser storage strictly to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Strictly Necessary Storage: Essential for fundamental page navigation, securing interactive consultation forms, and maintaining session state.
  • Preference & Theme Cookies: Stores user-selected theme preferences (such as our Full Black Theme or Full Yellow Theme) so your preferred visual ambiance is preserved across page visits.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Gathers aggregated, non-personally identifiable metrics on page visit velocity, portfolio image engagement, and popular services across Peshawar neighborhoods to help us optimize server performance.

3. Third-Party Integrations

Our website may embed interactive content, video testimonial players, and map directions. These third-party services (such as Google Fonts or WhatsApp messaging integrations) may set their own cookies according to their independent privacy standards.

4. Managing & Disabling Cookies

You can choose to disable, filter, or purge cookies through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). Please note that disabling essential storage may impact your ability to retain custom theme states or interactive cost calculation summaries.
