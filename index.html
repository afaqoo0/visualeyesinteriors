Frequently asked questions

Can we schedule a site visit? +
Absolutely! We encourage site visits to truly understand your space, lighting, and layout possibilities. Contact our team to book a convenient time.
Do you have experience in interior design project management? +
Yes, we provide end-to-end project management. From initial concept to the final handover, our dedicated team ensures everything is executed flawlessly and on time.
Do you have an in-house team? +
Yes, we have a complete in-house team of highly skilled designers, architects, and project managers to guarantee the highest quality for your project.
Do you have partnerships with contractors? +
We collaborate with a trusted network of premium contractors and artisans in Dubai who meet our strict quality and reliability standards.
Do you offer both design and fit-out services? +
Yes! We provide turnkey solutions. We handle both the creative design process and the complete fit-out execution to bring the vision to reality.
Where do you source your items from? +
We source globally and locally. Depending on your design style and budget, we procure high-end materials and custom furniture from trusted international brands and bespoke local artisans.
Do you work with the dubai authorities as well? +
Yes, we handle all necessary paperwork and approvals with Dubai Municipality, Civil Defense, and building managements (NOCs) to ensure full compliance.
Will I be assigned a dedicated designer once i give you the project? +
Certainly. You will have a dedicated lead designer and project manager assigned exclusively to you to ensure clear communication and a seamless journey.
What are your project management fees? +
Our project management fees are structured transparently and vary depending on the scope and complexity of your project. We provide a clear breakdown during our initial proposal.
What is the timeline for the design? +
A standard design phase takes between 3 to 6 weeks, depending on the project's size, detail level, and the speed of revisions and feedback.
Will I receive a refund if i don't like the design? +
We work closely with you through mood boards and concepts before finalizing. While design fees are generally non-refundable due to the time invested, we guarantee revisions until you are completely satisfied with the result.

A special note from founder

Still unsure? start with interior design consultation

I have lost count of how many people reach me halfway through a renovation they no longer recognise. They started full of ideas, spoke to two or three firms, and somewhere the project stopped feeling like theirs. Early on I learned this the hard way. I once gave a client exactly what they asked for instead of what they needed, and we both watched a beautiful space fall flat. The right design starts with the right conversation, not the right Pinterest board.

So here is my honest advice. If you are still unsure whether you need an interior design consultant or a full interior designer, start with a consultation. It costs less, commits you to nothing, and gives you clarity before you spend on the wrong thing.

And right now you speak directly to me, the founder and principal designer, not a junior reading from a script. Whether you are here in Dubai or buying from another country, I will tell you exactly where to begin. 10 years, 100+ interior design projects, and not one left unfinished.

Haris
